Privacy Policy

Updated November 19, 2026

In short: ShotBasket collects no data and makes no network connections. Your screenshots stay on your Mac until you paste, drag or share them yourself.

No data collection, no network connections

ShotBasket has no accounts, cloud uploads, advertising, analytics, tracking code or server run by us. It does not connect to the internet, and nothing you capture is sent to us.

Screenshots on your Mac

ShotBasket keeps your screenshots on your Mac so you can collect, edit and paste them as separate files. It uses screen access only for the captures you ask for. The input permission is used for the capture shortcuts and for pasting. ShotBasket does not record what you type, never presses Return and never sends messages.

  • Screenshots in the basket are kept across restarts and are never deleted automatically.
  • A screenshot that leaves the basket stays available for at least ten minutes, or the longer delay you choose, so the app you pasted into can finish reading it. Cleanup runs while ShotBasket is open or the next time it opens.
  • Copies saved to a folder you choose stay there, unless you turn on a deletion period for new copies ShotBasket saves. Changing the folder, turning off saving or turning off the deletion period stops that. Files ShotBasket did not create, or that were changed later, are never deleted.
  • Editing creates a new PNG. It does not change the original or copies saved earlier. Editable project files you export contain the original image and your edits, and are never pasted as basket attachments.

Text recognition and search

Reading text and QR codes in a screenshot happens on your Mac. Searching past captures is off until you turn it on, and then covers new captures only. The search index stores the recognized text and references to the files, without extra copies of the images. Turning search off clears the recognized text.

Sharing you start

When you choose Share, macOS shows its standard share menu and passes the selected images to the service you pick, such as AirDrop, Messages or Mail. That service handles the files under its own terms. ShotBasket never shares anything automatically.

Your choices

You can pause the shortcuts, clear the basket, change how long screenshots are kept after they leave the basket, and remove ShotBasket’s permissions in System Settings at any time. Files or text you copy stay on the macOS clipboard until something else replaces them. How the app you paste into reads and shows attachments is up to that app.

Children

ShotBasket collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

ShotBasket is free to download. The 3-day trial and the full version are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple through the Mac App Store. ShotBasket only learns from macOS whether you own them; it never sees your payment details or Apple Account.

This website

The shotbasket.com website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and does not follow you across other sites. It records page views with basic technical details such as the page, the referring site, the browser and the country. This applies to the website only: the ShotBasket app makes no network connections.
