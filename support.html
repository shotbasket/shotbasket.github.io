Common problems

The shortcuts open the normal macOS screenshot

ShotBasket needs the Accessibility permission to take over ⌘⇧4, ⌘⇧3, ⌘⇧2 and ⌘V. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn ShotBasket on. On macOS 27 this section is called Device Control and Data Access. Then click the ShotBasket icon in the menu bar and choose Enable Shortcuts.

Screenshots come out empty or show only the desktop

Turn ShotBasket on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording (Screen & System Audio Recording on newer macOS versions). macOS may ask you to quit and reopen the app before the change takes effect.

⌘V pasted my normal clipboard

  • The basket was empty. ShotBasket only takes over ⌘V when it has screenshots waiting.
  • You copied something after your last capture. The newest thing wins: that ⌘V pastes what you copied and keeps the basket, and the next ⌘V pastes the basket.
  • The shortcuts are paused. Choose Enable Shortcuts from the menu bar icon.
  • A password field is active somewhere. macOS blocks shortcut handling while secure input is on. Click outside the password field, or close the app that is holding it, and try again.

Only one image arrived, or the files arrived as text

The app you pasted into decides what it accepts. Some fields take a single image or only text. Try the attachment area of the same app, or drag the capture from the notch into it instead.

Trial and purchase

What happens when the trial ends?

Your shortcuts go back to the normal macOS behaviour, and ShotBasket’s menu offers the full version. Captures already in the basket are kept. Unlock the full version once to carry on.

Using ShotBasket on another Mac

Sign in to the Mac App Store with the same Apple Account, open ShotBasket’s Settings and click Restore Purchases.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

More

I want to see the introduction again

Click the ShotBasket icon in the menu bar and choose Guide. It walks through permissions, capturing, editing and pasting.

Removing ShotBasket

Click the ShotBasket icon in the menu bar, choose Quit, then move ShotBasket from Applications to the Trash. You can also remove it from the permission lists in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
